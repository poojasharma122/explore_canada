About Banff National Park

Banff National Park is one of Canada’s most iconic travel destinations, known for its stunning mountain scenery, crystal-clear lakes, wildlife, and outdoor adventures. Located in the heart of the Canadian Rockies, Banff attracts travelers from around the world looking for nature, relaxation, and adventure.

Lake Louise

Famous for its turquoise water surrounded by majestic mountain peaks.

Moraine Lake

One of the most photographed destinations in Canada with breathtaking natural beauty.

Banff Gondola

Enjoy panoramic mountain views from the top of Sulphur Mountain.

Johnston Canyon

Explore waterfalls, hiking trails, and scenic canyon pathways.

Bow Falls

A beautiful waterfall located near Banff town with stunning mountain surroundings.

Things to Do in Banff

Hiking

Explore scenic mountain trails with breathtaking views.

Wildlife Viewing

See elk, deer, bears, and other Canadian wildlife.

Canoeing

Enjoy peaceful canoe rides on beautiful lakes.

Skiing & Snowboarding

Experience world-class winter sports in Banff resorts.

Photography

Capture stunning landscapes and unforgettable scenery.

Best Time to Visit Banff

🌿 Summer

Perfect for hiking, sightseeing, canoeing, and road trips.

❄️ Winter

Ideal for skiing, snowboarding, and snowy adventures.

🍂 Fall

Enjoy colorful autumn scenery and fewer tourists.

🌸 Spring

Experience fresh mountain landscapes and peaceful nature.

Travel Tips for Banff Visitors

  • Carry warm clothing during mountain trips
  • Book hotels early during peak season
  • Start hikes early for the best experience
  • Respect wildlife and park regulations
  • Keep a camera for scenic photography
