About Niagara Falls

Niagara Falls is one of the most famous natural attractions in the world, attracting millions of visitors every year. Located on the border of Canada and the United States, Niagara Falls is known for its breathtaking waterfalls, scenic views, exciting attractions, and unforgettable travel experiences.

Horseshoe Falls

Experience the largest and most powerful waterfall of the Canadian side of Niagara Falls.

Journey Behind the Falls

Explore tunnels behind the waterfall and witness the incredible power of the falls.

Niagara SkyWheel

Enjoy panoramic views of Niagara Falls and the surrounding city from above.

Clifton Hill

Discover entertainment, restaurants, attractions, and family-friendly experiences.

Niagara Boat Tour

Get close to the waterfalls with an unforgettable boat adventure experience.

Travel Tips for Niagara Falls Visitors

  • Carry waterproof clothing for boat tours
  • Visit early to avoid large crowds
  • Keep a camera for scenic photography
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes
  • Explore nearby attractions and restaurants
